Since 2019

Work in Ghana

Supporting the development of mental health care in Ghana, West Africa.

Since 2019 Peter has been working in Ghana to support the development of mental health care in community health clinics and in the prison services of Ghana. It builds on a long record of international mental health service development, including work for Iraq and Egypt.

Working with schools

Peter is now actively supporting three schools in Ghana with educational programmes on the mental health and wellbeing of pupils and teachers:

  • Optimum Prime School Complex in Kasoa
  • Mount Sinai Senior High School in Akropong
  • Zion College High School (ZICO) in Anloga, Volta Region

Working with the Ghana Prison Service at Nsawam

The work at Nsawam, led by Dangana Foundation{% include new-tab.html %}, includes a weekly addiction programme co-produced by former prisoners. The work is now extending to prepare prisoners for their release into the community.

Dangana Foundation

Peter is an Associate Consultant with Dangana Foundation{% include new-tab.html %}, a licensed health and social care service in Ghana focused on mental health. Dangana works across Ghana, particularly in the Greater Accra, Ashanti, Northern and Western regions, and its vision is to inspire a cultural shift in Ghana from dependence to independence.

Dangana's approach is built on co-design, inclusivity and creativity, and its team includes people with lived experience of mental health challenges. Its work includes:

  • peer support and peer group facilitation
  • bespoke and accredited training, including leadership programmes, Recovery Star and Mental Health First Aid
  • workplace mental health and wellbeing training
  • mental health rehabilitation programmes
  • criminal justice and substance misuse programmes

Visit dangana.org{% include new-tab.html %}
