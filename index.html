About Peter

Peter has over 40 years' experience in the NHS working at executive level. As Director of Nursing and Modernisation he provided the leadership for transformational change in mental health services at Kent and Medway NHS and Social Care Partnership Trust. Peter also worked for three years in commissioning mental health, forensic and high secure services across Kent, Surrey and Sussex.

A Registered Mental Health Nurse, Peter has a proven track record of developing inter-agency working, tackling discrimination in mental health, developing nationally recognised award-winning services, and international service development for Iraq, Egypt and Ghana. Peter lectures on mental health, dementia care and leadership, and maintains a high national profile.

Areas of expertise

  • National leadership

    Peter has co-ordinated the work programme for the National Mental Health and Learning Disability Nurse Directors Forum since 2009, and is actively involved in mental health and learning disability activity across England. He facilitates a national Aspiring Director leadership programme in conjunction with the NHS Confederation.

  • Executive coaching

    Peter trained as an executive coach in 2014 and coaches both individuals and groups, with particular emphasis on senior clinical and managerial leadership and career development.

  • Inquiries and service reviews

    Peter has undertaken many serious incident inquiries for NHS Trusts, including some high-profile cases, as well as clinical service reviews for NHS Trusts and Social Services. He has also worked as a professional advisor for the CQC.

  • Voluntary Role

    Peter joined the national learning disability and mental health charity Choice Support as a trustee in 2016, and chaired its Quality and Safeguarding committee until 2025, when he completed his nine-year tenure.

    In 2025 Peter became the chair of the Patient Participation Group (PPG) for his local GP surgery, which works in partnership as a critical friend to improve primary care services in the community.

Mental health in Ghana

Since 2019 Peter has been working in Ghana, West Africa, to support the development of mental health care in community health clinics and in the prison services of Ghana. He is now also supporting three schools with educational programmes on the mental health and wellbeing of pupils and teachers.

He is an Associate Consultant with Dangana{% include new-tab.html %}, a licensed health and social care service in Ghana focused on mental health. Dangana works across Greater Accra, Ashanti, Northern and Western regions, with a mission of supporting people in their transition from dependence to independence through peer support, training and rehabilitation programmes.
